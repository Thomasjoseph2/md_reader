# Privacy policy: Markdown Folder Viewer

*Last updated 16 November 2026 (share links added).*

Reading files does not collect, store, send or sell any personal data. Sharing a link is optional and described below.

- The files you open are read only inside your browser, on your computer. They are uploaded only if you choose to create a share link (below).
- The extension makes no network requests. All of its code is inside the extension.
- There are no analytics and no tracking. Reading and reviewing need no account.
- A few things are saved in your browser only: light or dark mode, your reviewer name, your review comments (so they
  are not lost if the tab closes before you download them) and, in Chrome and Edge, which folder you opened last.
  You can clear them by removing the extension or clearing site data.
- Review files you download are saved to your own computer. You decide who to send them to.
- The extension reads `.md` files you open in Chrome from your computer (`file://` addresses), only to show them
  formatted. It does not read any other pages.

## Share links (website only, optional)

- Clicking **Share** asks you to sign in with Google. We store your Google account id, email address, name and picture,
  and a login session. We use them only to run sign-in and to check who may open a link.
- Creating a link uploads the Markdown text of the open folder (not images) to our database. It is deleted when the link
  expires (1, 7 or 30 days, your choice) or when you remove it.
- A link is either open to anyone who has it (optionally with a password) or limited to the Google emails you add.
  People you add see the doc after signing in with that email.
- Passwords are stored only as salted hashes. The Markdown text itself is not encrypted from us, so do not share
  secrets this way.
- Sign-in uses one cookie (`mdv_sid`) to keep you logged in for 30 days. Signing out deletes the session.
- To have your account and every link removed, open an issue below.

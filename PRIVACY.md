# Privacy policy: Markdown Folder Viewer

*Last updated 9 November 2026.*

Markdown Folder Viewer does not collect, store, send or sell any personal data.

- The files you open are read only inside your browser, on your computer. They are never uploaded anywhere.
- The extension makes no network requests. All of its code is inside the extension.
- There are no analytics, no tracking and no accounts.
- Two settings are saved in your browser only: light or dark mode, and (in Chrome and Edge) which folder you opened
  last, so you can reopen it. You can clear them by removing the extension or clearing site data.
- The extension reads `.md` files you open in Chrome from your computer (`file://` addresses), only to show them
  formatted. It does not read any other pages.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/Thomasjoseph2/md_reader/issues
